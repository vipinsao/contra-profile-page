The brief

The problem

Retreat hosts sell a handful of rooms at different prices, often by email and bank transfer. Kajabi can take payments, but it has no room booking built in, and a sold-out room must never be sold twice.

The idea

Use what Kajabi already does well. One Offer per room type with a quantity limit, custom checkout fields for the details a host needs, and automations for everything after payment. The guest just sees one calm page.

How the story plays

Beat by beat.

01 · 0:04

A landing page built from the owner’s content

Hero, dates, what’s included and the rooms, in a quiet editorial style that matches a countryside retreat: “Three quiet days in the Cotswolds.”

02 · 0:09

Room selection with real availability

Private room, shared twin priced per bed, and an ensuite suite that is sold out and sends people to a waitlist instead of a dead end.

03 · 0:16

Register and pay in one flow

The existing Kajabi checkout, with custom fields for dietary needs and arrival, and a clear order summary beside it.

04 · 0:25

The confirmation sends itself

Payment received, guest tagged, confirmation email sent, and a reminder with a packing list goes out 7 days before.

05 · 0:30

Booked from a phone, in a minute

Mobile-first with a sticky Reserve bar, checked on iPhone SE to Pro Max, Pixel, iPad and desktop browsers.
