The brief

The problem

Habit apps lose most people in the first week. “Retro” is also easy to get wrong: lean too far and it looks like a costume instead of a product people trust every morning.

The idea

Design around one daily loop (check in, streak, reward, review) and make the day-7 moment feel earned. Keep the retro warmth in color and type, and keep the structure as disciplined as a modern fitness app.

How the story plays
