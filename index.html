The 5-second test

What a viewer decides, second by second.

Most people choose to watch or scroll before the fifth second. Every video I make is built around these five beats. Run the clock or drag the playhead.

0.0s
0s
1s
2s
3s
4s
0 – 1s

Hook

A first frame that moves. It has to stop the thumb before anything else can happen.

1 – 2s

Problem

Name the pain in the viewer's own words, so they know this is for them.

2 – 3s

Product

Show the product doing the job. Real interface, one action, no feature list.

3 – 4s

Proof

One clear outcome on screen: the chart goes up, the task gets done.

4 – 5s

Action

Tell them exactly what to do next, while you still have their attention.

Your product gets 5 seconds. Let's use all of them.
